# Personas 

Below you will find two complete user persona profiles, structured and ready for UX research documentation: María García and Javier López. Each profile follows the required framework for UX research.

---

## Persona: María García
- Name: María García
- Age: 34
- Gender: Female
- Profession: University Professor (Humanities)
- Nationality: Spanish
- City / Location: Madrid (visits Granada for tourism)

### Biography / Lifestyle
María works at the university and travels frequently for cultural and culinary reasons. She plans her outings in advance, checks reviews, and pays close attention to food quality and safety. She usually travels with a partner or in small groups and values places with a good reputation and atmosphere.

### Quote
"I prefer to plan ahead: I want to know if I can eat safely and enjoy myself without surprises."

### GOALS / INTERESTS
- Find dining options that respect her dietary restrictions (allergens / plant-based options).
- Book tables at venues with a good reputation and close cultural atmosphere.
- Access clear information about menus, ingredients, and prices before deciding.
- Minimize search time and avoid unnecessary wait times.
- Discover authentic local recommendations.

### PAIN POINTS / CONCERNS
- Incomplete information regarding ingredients or allergens.
- Unclear or inconsistent pricing.
- Long or confusing reservation processes.
- Poorly optimized mobile interfaces or intrusive popups.
- Lack of real reviews or trustworthy photos.

### SCENARIO
During a weekend trip to Granada, María is looking for a dinner spot that combines good food and a local atmosphere; she compares reviews, checks the menu on her smartphone, and wants to book a table without uncertainty regarding ingredients or prices.

### MOTIVATIONS
- Achievement / progress: Medium
- Security / stability: High
- Comfort / ease: High

### SKILLS
- Technology: Medium (uses apps and web proficiently, prefers clear interfaces)
- Problem-solving: Medium-High (organized and analytical)

### PERSONALITY
- Introverted — Extroverted: Introverted (tends to plan ahead and avoids major improvisations)
- Low confidence — High confidence: Medium
- Follower — Leader: Follower
- Closed — Open to ideas: Open to ideas (if well-justified)
- Works alone — Collaborative: Collaborative (travels in pairs/small groups)

### BRANDS
- Google Maps / Google Reviews
- Instagram
- TripAdvisor / TheFork
- Reservation apps (TheFork / local platforms)

## Persona: Javier López
- Name: Javier López
- Age: 26
- Gender: Male
- Profession: Entrepreneur (local startup) / Occasional Community Manager
- Nationality: Spanish
- City / Location: Granada (resident)

### Biography / Lifestyle
Javier is a young entrepreneur fond of gastronomy and sharing plans on social media. He goes out frequently with friends, looks for spots with good value for money, and values speed in orders and the ability to share experiences online.

### Quote
"If I can order and arrive on time with the perfect meal, it's a winning plan."

### GOALS / INTERESTS
- Find new burger joints and culinary experiences to share.
- Book or order quickly from a mobile phone.
- Find attractive options for groups (meal deals and offers).
- Share photos and reviews on social media.
- Earn loyalty benefits or discounts.

### PAIN POINTS / CONCERNS
- Slow ordering/booking processes with too many steps.
- Lack of clear information on delivery or pickup times.
- Prices and options poorly visible on mobile.
- Uncomfortable checkout experience (multiple forms, no quick-pay options).
- Deficient packaging or presentation for takeaway orders.

### SCENARIO
Planning an outing with friends: he looks for a venue or takeaway order, compares times and prices on his phone, and needs to confirm the order in under 5 minutes to avoid wasting social time.

### MOTIVATIONS
- Achievement / progress: Medium
- Security / stability: Medium
- Comfort / ease: High

### SKILLS
- Technology: High (very comfortable using mobile apps and social networks)
- Problem-solving: Medium (pragmatic, prioritizes quick solutions)

### PERSONALITY
- Introverted — Extroverted: Extroverted
- Low confidence — High confidence: High confidence
- Follower — Leader: Leader (usually proposes plans and organizes groups)
- Closed — Open to ideas: Open to ideas
- Works alone — Collaborative: Collaborative

### BRANDS
- Instagram
- Glovo / JustEat / Deliveroo
- Goiko (and other local burger restaurants)
- Spotify (for playlists during outings)
- X (Twitter) / TikTok (for trends and discoveries)
