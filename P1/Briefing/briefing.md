# Executive Briefing 

To evaluate the usability and user experience of Goiko's website, we conducted a comprehensive battery of tests that allowed us to identify both key strengths and critical areas for improvement.

First, we defined a **User Research Plan** to outline the analytical approach, key evaluation criteria, and the most relevant tests for this context.

Next, we carried out a **Competitive Analysis**, benchmarking Goiko against competitors such as Sancho and Capital Burger. The primary finding was that Goiko stands out for its interface clarity and organization, yet lags behind in providing actionable information for decision-making, particularly regarding price visibility.

Following this, we developed two **user personas** and their corresponding **journey maps** to simulate distinct usage patterns. The first profile prioritizes clear information on ingredients and allergens; the second focuses on speed when booking and browsing the menu. This exercise validated several necessary improvements: enhancing the visibility of prices and allergens, streamlining booking and payment processes, and organizing the menu more coherently—including better options for groups.

Finally, we applied a **Usability Review**, which reinforced our prior findings and uncovered new opportunities, particularly concerning mobile experience and the integration of immediate call-to-actions (such as booking or ordering).

Overall, the evaluation demonstrates a solid foundation in visual clarity, alongside concrete modifications that could significantly improve the user experience and, consequently, customer conversion rates.
