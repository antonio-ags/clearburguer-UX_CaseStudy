# Competitive Analysis — Goiko vs. Sancho & Capital Burger

---

## Overview

This analysis evaluates the competitive position of **Goiko** in the gourmet burger market, comparing it against two direct benchmarks within the same segment.

### Selected Competitors

| Brand | Website | Coverage |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Sancho Casual Burger** | https://sanchocasualburger.com/ | National |
| **Capital Burger** | https://capitalburger.es/ | National |

> These competitors were chosen because they are businesses similar to ours with comparable value propositions in terms of features and user experience.

---

## Methodology

### Evaluation Criteria

A series of criteria related to **user experience** were established, evaluated based on:

- **Rating scale:** Low / Medium / High
- **Binary criteria:** Yes / No (presence/absence of functionality)

### Key Aspects Analyzed

The criteria were selected in direct alignment with the User Research Plan, allowing us to evaluate:

- Information and features available on each platform
- Clarity and accessibility of information
- Visual design and content organization
- Purchase/booking experience
- Responsiveness and usability across devices

---

## Main Findings

### Goiko's Strengths

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Visual clarity** | Flawless structure in information presentation |
| **Intuitive navigation** | Well-structured hierarchical menu |
| **Consistency** | Coherent experience across sections |

### Identified Gaps vs. Competitors

| Competitor | Advantage | Impact on Goiko |
|------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Sancho** | More detailed information | Need to improve price clarity |
| **Capital Burger** | Attractive visual atmosphere | Product presentation can be improved |
| **Both** | Prominent pricing data | Critical gap: lack of price visibility |

### Critical Gap Detected

**Lack of visible pricing on Goiko's platform**

Users cannot easily find pricing information across most views. This directly impacts purchasing decisions and represents a competitive advantage for Sancho and Capital Burger.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### To Improve Competitiveness

1. **Feature prices prominently**
   - Update product cards with clear pricing
   - Display pricing clearly on the online menu and promotional landing pages

2. **Incorporate visual elements inspired by Capital Burger**
   - Upgrade product photography
   - Add visual context (venue atmosphere, final dishes)

3. **Maintain our core strengths**
   - Preserve our current clarity and organization (existing advantage)
   - Expand features without sacrificing usability

4. **Learn from Sancho's practices**
   - Provide comprehensive documentation (allergens, ingredients)
   - Streamline access to booking details

---

## General Conclusion

**Goiko's current standing:** Strong information clarity and navigation, but needs to strengthen price visibility and improve visual product presentation to compete effectively.

Combining our structural clarity with Capital Burger's visual atmosphere and Sancho's detailed documentation would position Goiko as a leader in its segment.
