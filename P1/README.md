# Usability & UX Research Analysis

---

## 1. User Research Plan

**Objective:** Establish the research framework, methodology, and goals for Goiko's usability analysis.

The plan defines the research strategy surrounding Goiko's website (https://www.goiko.com/es/carta/hamburguesas/), focusing on enhancing the user experience and increasing conversion rates. Key functions to evaluate have been identified: making a reservation, checking store locations, and accessing pricing, ingredient, and allergen information.

**Proposed methodology:** A combination of qualitative techniques (user interviews) and quantitative techniques (timed task testing, measurement of time and errors). Participants of various ages and levels of experience with restaurant platforms will be recruited.

**Expected outcome:** A usability report identifying key areas for improvement.

## 2. Competitive Analysis

**Objective:** Evaluate Goiko's market position relative to alternatives to identify opportunities for improvement and core strengths to maintain.

A comparative analysis was performed between Goiko and two direct competitors: **Sancho Casual Burger** (https://sanchocasualburger.com/) and **Capital Burger** (https://capitalburger.es/). Criteria aligned with the research plan were evaluated: information and features, clarity and accessibility, visual design, and overall experience.

**Key findings:**
- Goiko stands out in **clarity and information organization** compared to the competition.
- Both alternative platforms present **similar functionalities** (reservations, online ordering, locations).
- **Identified gap:** Lack of pricing information on Goiko, which appears clearly on competitor sites.
- **Opportunity:** Incorporate successful visual patterns from Capital Burger (atmosphere and product presentation).

## 3. Personas & User Journey Maps

**Objective:** Create research-backed user archetypes to simulate real interactions with the platform and uncover profile-specific problems.

Two fictional personas representing key user segments have been developed:

### Persona 1: María García (Cautious Planner)
- **Profile:** University professor, 34 years old, cultural traveler with a strong interest in food safety.
- **Primary motivation:** Find dining options that respect dietary restrictions (allergens) and offer clear information on prices and ingredients.
- **Pain points:** Incomplete allergen information, lack of visible prices, confusing reservation processes.
- **Scenario:** During a weekend trip to Granada, she looks for a quality dinner spot with a local atmosphere; she compares reviews and needs to confirm availability without uncertainty regarding ingredients or prices.

### Persona 2: Javier López (Agile Explorer)
- **Profile:** Young entrepreneur, 26 years old, resident in Granada with a passion for gastronomy and social media.
- **Primary motivation:** Quick outings with friends; seeks attractive alternatives, group packages, and social sharing capabilities.
- **Pain points:** Slow ordering/booking processes, lack of clear information on delivery times, uncomfortable checkout experience, poor packaging on takeaway orders.
- **Scenario:** Plans a social outing; needs to confirm location, pay, and coordinate in under 5 minutes from his mobile device.

## 4. Usability Review

**Objective:** Systematically evaluate Goiko's usability using a best-practice checklist to identify concrete deficiencies and strengths.

The **Usability Review** methodology (based on "UX for the Masses") was applied through a structured evaluation table. Aspects such as features, navigation, design, performance, and content were assessed against recognized usability standards.

**Global result:** **Score of 70/100 (GOOD)**

**Strengths:**
- Clean visual design with good use of white space.
- Basic functionalities present and accessible.

**Critical improvement areas:**
- Mobile optimization (suboptimal experience on small devices).
- Lack of clear information on allergens and prices across most views.

- [Usability Review Report](UsabilityReview/Usability-review.pdf)

## 5. Briefing

**Objective:** Summarize main findings and conclusions for stakeholder communication.

Following the comprehensive evaluation of Goiko through UX research, competitive analysis, user modeling, and usability testing, the platform shows solid foundations in clarity and organization, but possesses **critical opportunities for improvement** regarding product information, mobile experience, and transaction speed.

**Conclusion:** Implementing visible pricing and allergen information, optimizing for mobile, and streamlining booking/payment processes would significantly increase conversion rates and user satisfaction for both studied user profiles.
